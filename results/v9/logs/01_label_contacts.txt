How to read this:
 * 'near brain' = in the brain or within 5 mm of it. The outer contacts of depth (stereo-EEG) electrodes
   sit in the skull, so 70-100% is normal; those contacts are not used for training. If it is very low
   (< 50%), the coordinates are in the wrong space: try COORD_SPACE = 'tkr' in config.py.
 * 'near GM' = contacts on or within a few mm of a grey-matter region. Depth electrodes have
   many contacts in white matter, so 40-80% is normal. Only these contacts get a region.
 * 'L/R matches name': contacts named L... should be in the left hemisphere, R... in the right.
   If this is near 0%, left and right are flipped somewhere. 'n/a' = names have no L/R prefix.
 * 'uncertain' sites strongly start a seizure but are not SOZ (e.g. a one-off start): they are used neither
   as SOZ nor as 'not SOZ' (left out of the patches, and their region gets no label unless it has a SOZ site).
