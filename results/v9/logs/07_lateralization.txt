How to read this:
 * 'majority' always says the more common side: any useful model must beat its balanced accuracy (0.5).
 * balanced accuracy = average of 'left patients correct' and 'right patients correct'; AUROC = the fair
   score that does not depend on a model's lean to one side (0.5 = guessing).
 * With ~25 patients one patient = 4%, so differences of a few percent are not meaningful.
